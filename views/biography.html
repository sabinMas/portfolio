About Me

Welcome fellow traveler. I'm endlessly curious about how things work, are you not? From mechanical machines and lines of code to the deeper stuff like human behavior and the many intelligences of the world whether natural or artificial. As of 2025 I am pursuing my BAS in Software Development and an associate degree in Data Analytics, spending most of my time working hands-on with Java, Python, SQL, and front-end design.

I combine analytical problem-solving techniques with imaginative thinking to build solutions that both function well and look unique. As an artist and a designer, I aim to deliver work that not only meets expectations but surpasses them. I Spend most of my free-time outdoors in nature learning and drawing inspiration from the ecosystems that make our planet so astounding.

At 14 I started my first job at Ben Franklin Crafts, where I learned more about art and design as well as time management. After graduating high school I spent 2 years on a religious mission in Southern Italy and Sicily. While in Italy, I volunteered as a live interpreter for African refugees, taught English in refugee camps, and took on a leadership role in the transition of missionary work to digital platforms during the COVID-19 pandemic. Our team of missionaries launched regional Italian social media pages and expanded virtual English courses, seeing record participation numbers. This helped establish these practices permanently even after the pandemic!

Upon returning to Washington, I continued my creative work: designing music album covers, small-business logo's, business card's, 3D animations for music promos, as well as designs for a local small clothing brand. All while working in construction and doing hydro-excavation I learned first hand the importance of eco-concious infrastructure practices. After a winter layoff, I decided to return to school and focus full-time on tech, design, and data.
